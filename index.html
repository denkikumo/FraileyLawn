Why Fall Is the Best Time to Seed: Warm soil and cool air are ideal for germination, weed competition is low, and new grass has time to root before winter — so it fills in fast and comes back strong in spring. The catch: the fall window is short. New grass needs several weeks of growing weather to establish before the first hard frost, so the sooner it goes down, the better it takes. Can't get to it this fall? I seed in spring too — but if your lawn needs help now, fall is the better window to use.
